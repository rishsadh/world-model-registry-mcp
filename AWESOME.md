# Verified world models

An awesome-style list of world models and scene generators. Every line carries the date the registry read the vendor's pages, the commercial-use state, and whether the source links answered. It is generated from the [World Model Registry](https://rishsadh.com/world-model-registry) by `world-model-registry-mcp awesome` and is not edited by hand.

Registry version 2, last verified 2026-09-29. Links checked 2026-09-30: 135 URLs, 133 live, 1 blocked, 1 dead, 0 errored.

How to read a line. The commercial state is one of quoted-grant, licence-named, conditions, conflict or not-published. `conflict` means a clause or a source says no, or two sources disagree. `blocked` means a site refused the automated check, not that the page is gone. A live link does not prove the quoted text is still on the page. This is not legal advice and it has no scores or rankings.
